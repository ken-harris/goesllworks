Multi Timer privacy policy

Goes'll Works · Effective October 16, 2026

The short version: Multi Timer doesn't collect, share, or sell any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app doesn't have permission to use the internet. Your timers stay on your phone.

What the app stores

To work, Multi Timer saves the things you create on your device only:

  • Your timer groups and timers: names, emoji icons, durations, messages, and alert settings
  • Which timers are running and when they end, so alerts still go off if the app is closed or the phone restarts
  • Your chosen alert sound, saved as a reference to a sound already on your phone. Sound files are never copied or uploaded.

This information never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it anytime by deleting groups or timers in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If you've turned on backup for your phone, Android may include Multi Timer's settings in your Google account backup so they can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. Goes'll Works can't see or access it.

Permissions

Multi Timer asks only for what it needs to be a reliable timer. None of these give it access to your personal information.

NotificationsShow running timers with Restart and Stop buttons, and alert you when a timer finishes.
AlarmsMake timers go off at exactly the right time, even when your phone is asleep.
Run at startupPut running timers back after your phone restarts or the app updates.
VibrationVibrate when a timer finishes.

Children

Multi Timer doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
